## Pitch angle
1. No website: the salon exists online only as a Facebook feed and directory scrapes.
2. Listings disagree: two sets of hours (Google 10–5 / Sat 8–12 vs Facebook & Yelp 9–7 / Fri–Sat 9–3), an old phone number (366-9540) still listed, and a California salon's sign sitting in their photo gallery.
3. Nothing tells a new client what they can book: no service list, no team page, no prices, no payment info; new-stylist announcements (Ashley, Jennie's openings) scroll away in the feed.
4. "Call or walk in" is the only path; a site can make that one tap and prep the call.
