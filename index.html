Our owner

Rosie Keller has been doing hair since 1969.

Ten years at Jean Paige, four at The Main Event, then 36 years running her own salon, Crystal Rose. In 2018 she opened Beauty Bar on the Boulevard and filled it with stylists, a nail tech and an esthetician.
