Questions

Do you take walk-ins?

Yes. Walk on in any day we’re open. If you’re after a particular stylist or a longer service like color, calling ahead is the surest way to get a chair.

How much does it cost?

Our prices aren’t online yet. Call (765) 365-5000 and we’ll give you a quote for what you have in mind.

Can I book a specific stylist?

Yes, ask for them by name. Ashley also takes bookings on her direct line, 765-719-1140. See the team.

Do you cut kids’ hair?

Yes. Kids’ cuts are on our service list, and our Google profile lists us as good for kids.

Is the salon accessible?

Yes. The salon is wheelchair accessible.

Do you have Wi-Fi?

Yes, free Wi-Fi while you wait or sit under the dryer.
